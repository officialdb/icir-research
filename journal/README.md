## Purpose & Journaling Standards

The **ICIR Research Journal** serves as an intellectual log of the initiative. Science is non-linear; the purpose of this journal is to capture the genesis of ideas, the rationale behind architectural shifts, theoretical dead-ends, and preliminary reflections before they formalize into technical reports or registered experiments.

### Standards for Journal Entries
1. **Immutable Historical Record:** Entries reflect the author's state of knowledge at the time of writing. Past entries are never retroactively edited to look prescient.
2. **First-Principles Scrutiny:** Every entry must articulate the *why*—the conceptual or mathematical motivation driving an investigation.
3. **Cross-Referencing:** Entries must link to corresponding items in `experiments/`, `literature-reviews/`, or the topical notes directories.
