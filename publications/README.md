**Institute for Cognitive Intelligence Research (ICIR)**  
*Preprints, Technical Monographs, and Formal Working Papers*

---

## 1. Transparency & Publication Policy

The **Institute for Cognitive Intelligence Research (ICIR)** was established in October 2026 as an independent research initiative. In strict adherence to scientific integrity:

> **Important Notice:**  
> ICIR does not claim fictitious peer-reviewed journal publications, non-existent conference acceptances, or institutional academic accreditations. All scholarly works originating from this initiative are developed publicly, submitted to open preprint servers (e.g., arXiv), and archived here as open-access Technical Reports subject to community peer review.

---

## 2. Publication Categories

1. **ICIR Technical Reports (`TR-YYYY-NNN`):** In-depth research papers presenting novel theoretical models, architectural blueprints, or rigorous empirical benchmark evaluations. Every technical report must be accompanied by an executable reproducibility repository.
2. **Research Notes (`NOTE-*`):** Shorter conceptual or mathematical memos archived within topic directories.
3. **External Preprints & Submissions:** Formal papers prepared for submission to established computational cognitive science and machine learning venues (e.g., NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, Cognitive Science Society).
